# Feature Spec — Design-Practice Write-up (P2 Stretch)

## Status

Optional. Attempt only after the three project case studies and all P0 portfolio functions are complete and validated.

## Purpose

Show deliberate systems-design learning without presenting practice work as professional experience or a shipped production system.

## Eligible topics

- URL shortener
- Rate limiter

Choose one topic for the first write-up. A second is optional only if it does not delay core quality or deployment work.

## Required framing

- Section label: `DESIGN PRACTICE`
- Prominent qualifier: `Self-study exercise — not a production system.`
- Explain the problem, assumed constraints, a proposed architecture, key trade-offs, and open limitations.
- Separate assumptions from facts; metrics and scale figures must be clearly labeled as illustrative assumptions.

## Content and diagram rules

- Use an original, simple architecture diagram with accessible caption/text alternative.
- Cover one narrow scenario deeply instead of trying to imitate a full technical interview answer.
- Do not claim implementation, scale, deployment, users, benchmarks, or experience that does not exist.
- Do not place this section ahead of the real project case studies.

## Acceptance criteria

- The self-study qualifier is visible before any architectural claims.
- Assumptions, trade-offs, and limitations are clear.
- The diagram is legible at mobile width with a text equivalent.
- The write-up is omitted entirely if it is incomplete or weaker than the real project content.
