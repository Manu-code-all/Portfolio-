# Feature Spec — Achievements

## Purpose

Provide one concise, verifiable signal of extracurricular technical participation without overstating the result.

## Approved content

- **Title:** Smart India Hackathon — qualified team
- **Date:** September 2025
- **Description:** `Qualified and placed among the top 130 teams from 500+ teams in Smart India Hackathon 2025.`

## Content rules

- Use “qualified” and “top 130 of 500+ teams”; do not call this a win, finalist placement, national rank, or award unless a source confirms that wording.
- Do not invent a problem statement, project title, judging result, sponsor, team size, or external link.
- An external supporting link may be added only after it is verified and publicly accessible.

## Layout and accessibility

- Use a compact semantic article/list item with title, date, and description.
- Date must be text, not color or position alone.
- A modest index, rule, or date treatment is appropriate; do not use a trophy graphic or celebratory animation.

## Acceptance criteria

- The achievement is factually identical to the approved content above.
- It remains legible and unclipped at 375px width.
- Any optional link is verified before publication and opens safely in a new tab.
